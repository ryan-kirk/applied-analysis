# SSA Agricultural Capital Pressure

![Three-panel chart of SSA agriculture value added, imported-food share threshold windows, contextual events, and capital-cost proxy](ssa_agricultural_capital_pressure.png)

## What Changed In The Capability

This example adds the next layer after threshold detection: contextual events.

The prior agriculture example showed when the imported-food-share proxy moved above a 15% attention threshold. This version keeps that signal, turns the above-threshold runs into named pressure windows, and asks what else was happening around those periods.

## Pressure Windows

The threshold windows are the same core periods identified in the earlier example:

- 2011-2014
- 2016-2018

Those are not one-year spikes. They are sustained windows where the imported-food-share proxy stayed above the attention level.

## Why Events Help

The event list is intentionally small. It marks a few moments that may help explain the background conditions around the windows:

- the 2010-2011 global food price spike
- the 2011 Horn of Africa drought
- the 2014-2015 commodity downturn
- the 2016 foreign-exchange pressure period in several SSA economies
- the growing policy attention to mechanization and digital agriculture by 2018

These events do not prove why the proxy crossed the threshold. They help show what kinds of macro, climate, and intervention context surrounded the periods of pressure.

## Capital-Cost Context

The new capital-cost line adds another descriptive layer. In this example it is a simple proxy built from lending-rate series for Nigeria, South Africa, and Kenya. It shows that capital costs were elevated entering the first pressure window and remained high through much of the 2010s, even though the second threshold window happened after the proxy had eased from its 2012 high.

That matters because it suggests the threshold windows were not just about one financing variable. Imported-food pressure can remain elevated while financing conditions, trade conditions, and climate shocks interact.

## What To Notice

- threshold windows turn a noisy line into interpretable periods
- events provide context for those periods without turning the chart into a news feed
- capital costs add a second trend that can sharpen the story without overclaiming
- the timing relationships are useful for interpretation, not proof of causation

## Takeaway

The main analytical step forward is that threshold windows can now be read alongside explicit contextual events and a second domain trend. That makes the signal more useful for human interpretation while keeping the repository's core discipline: clear descriptive analysis, explicit assumptions, and no causal overclaiming.
