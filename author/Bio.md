# About
Ryan Kirk is a leader in digital agriculture and applied AI, known for
bridging the gap between advanced technology and real-world outcomes. As
former Head of Data Science at John Deere, he helped shape how data,
analytics, and AI are used to drive decisions across the agricultural
ecosystem. He holds a PhD in Human-Computer Interaction, with a focus on
decision support systems in veterinary and clinical settings. Today, he
focuses on how organizations can harness data and emerging technologies
to build more connected, intelligent, and resilient decision support
systems.
