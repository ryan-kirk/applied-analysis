## About the Site

Applied Analysis is a portfolio surface for analytical systems, software projects, and case-study writing. Project pages describe the applications themselves, while blog posts show the framing, interpretation, and public-facing explanation behind selected work.

The main goal of the site is straightforward: make the work easy to review, understand, and discuss. Interaction is available where useful, but the site is primarily designed as a professional portfolio rather than a community hub.

## What You Will Find Here

- portfolio entries for selected applications and systems
- public case studies drawn from analytical work
- examples of decision-support framing, applied AI, geospatial thinking, and workflow design
- a mix of public source-backed projects and private portfolio entries described at a high level
